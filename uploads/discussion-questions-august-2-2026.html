Sunday Sermons Discussion Questions

August 2, 2026 • Back to the Standard: What Is a Disciple? • Joel Ramsey

Use these questions in your Wednesday night life group. They are meant to spark honest conversation, not just review the sermon, so feel free to let the discussion breathe.

  1. Joel said Citipointe doesn't just want to build a crowd, it wants to build a community of disciples. What is the difference between attending a church and belonging to one, and which one better describes your own life right now?
  2. He described two cultural extremes: chasing success, money, and influence on one hand, and isolating from everyone else on the other. Where do you see yourself leaning, and why do you think that pull feels so strong?
  3. Starting September 2, every life group at Citipointe will meet on Wednesday nights, with a goal of growing from 7 to 12 groups to 100 groups across the city. Why do you think consistency and simplicity like this matter for building real community?
  4. Joel used the illustration of measuring a board from the original piece of wood rather than from each successive copy, so the size does not slowly drift. In what areas of your own faith might you be measuring your life against tradition or culture rather than against Jesus himself?
  5. Read Matthew 28:19-20. Jesus' commission was not just to gather people but to make disciples and teach them everything he commanded. What is one command of Jesus you know you have heard clearly but have been slow to actually obey?
  6. Read Luke 6:40. “A disciple is not above his teacher, but everyone when he is fully trained will be like his teacher.” Joel said discipleship is about likeness, not just information. Who has shaped you to look more like Jesus, and who are you currently investing in that way?
  7. Read 1 John 2:6. “Whoever says he abides in him ought to walk in the same way in which he walked.” What would it look like this week for you to walk the way Jesus walked in one specific relationship or situation?
  8. Joel defined a true disciple as someone who has fully surrendered their life to Jesus, obeys what he said, does what he did, and makes disciples. Which of those four (surrender, obey, do, make disciples) feels hardest for you right now, and why?
  9. He talked about gathering in homes, bearing one another's burdens, praying for needs, and taking communion together each week rather than only connecting on Sunday mornings. What is one real need in your life right now that this group could help carry?
  10. Joel warned about being a church that professes Jesus but lives like the world around it. What is one area where your life currently looks more like the surrounding culture than like Jesus, and what would it take to bring it back to the standard?
