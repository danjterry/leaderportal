Our Heart

Our Lifegroups exist to make disciples by gathering people around Jesus in homes throughout our city.

Following the example found in Scripture, we devote ourselves to God's Word, prayer, fellowship, the breaking of bread, and caring for one another — so that Christ is glorified and His love is made visible to the world.

The Table

Let's break bread together

“Every day they continued to meet together in the temple courts. They broke bread in their homes and ate together with glad and sincere hearts, praising God and enjoying the favor of all the people. And the Lord added to their number daily those who were being saved.” — Acts 2:46–47 (NIV)

We didn't invent this — we're simply following the example found in Scripture. Because something special happens when we gather in homes, around a table, with Christ at the center.
